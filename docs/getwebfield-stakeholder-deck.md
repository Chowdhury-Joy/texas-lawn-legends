## Slide 2 — Executive summary

**What we sell**  
A custom website + operations platform for lawn care, cleaning, roofing, and similar trades — not a template, not a page builder.

**Hero product:** **Tier 3 Full Ops** — $4,798 one-time build + $250/mo hosting  
Website, online booking, projects, proposals, invoices, crew scheduling, and client portal in one place.

**Four entry alternatives** for buyers not ready for $5k upfront:
- Own it Tier 1 ($987) · Tier 2 ($1,777)
- Rent it Tier 1 ($200 + $117/mo) · Tier 2 ($200 + $227/mo)

**Stage today:** Working product + demo hub + 111 automated tests. Manual billing, managed installs per client. SaaS after ~5 paying clients.

**Separate venture:** Bangladesh market runs its own pricing track (not covered here).

## Slide 3 — The problem

**Home-service owners lose money after the click.**

| Pain | Real-life example |
|------|-------------------|
| Pretty website, no bookings | Visitor leaves — owner never knows |
| Quoting by text/email | 10+ hrs/week on estimates that go nowhere |
| Tool sprawl | Website + Jobber + QuickBooks + spreadsheets ≈ **$150–350/mo** |
| Dropped follow-ups | Lead gets a price, never books — no system catches it |

**They don't need another brochure.** They need leads that book and a back office that doesn't eat the week.

## Slide 7 — Revenue model

**Two tracks**

| Track | Who it's for | How we get paid |
|-------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Own it** | Ready to buy the build | Setup (milestones 20/40/40) + monthly hosting |
| **Rent it** | Need lower upfront | $200 bank setup + monthly subscription in advance |

**Buy-out:** Rent → Own anytime at full published setup ($987 / $1,777). $200 not credited.

**Upsells (separate contracts):** SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, email — fixed monthly, no % of ad spend.

**Internal escalator (not public):** +25% per tier after every 3 new clients close.

## Slide 8 — Go-to-market

**Phase 1 — Now (0→5 clients)**
1. **Free demo preview** — 2-min form → working homepage preview (no pitch call required)
2. **Demo call** — flip industry pack on live product
3. **Close Tier 3** when pain = admin chaos; offer Tier 1/2 or Rent as alternatives
4. **Manual deploy** — one install per client, production checklist, demo hub off

**Phase 2 — After 5 clients**
- Repeatable onboarding · auto-billing · evaluate multi-tenant SaaS

**Channels (founding):** Direct outreach, referrals, local SEO/ads upsells on existing clients
