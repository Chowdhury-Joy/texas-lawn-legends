# Getwebfield — Pricing

All prices in **USD**. For businesses in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia.

Two ways to work with us: **Own it** (one-time purchase) or **Rent it** (monthly subscription on our hosting).

---

## Tiers

### Tier 1 — Website + CMS

Marketing website with admin panel to edit your own content, branding, SEO, services, and testimonials.

### Tier 2 — Website + Booking

Everything in Tier 1, plus online estimates, lead capture, and booking time slots.

### Tier 3 — Full Ops

Everything in Tier 2, plus projects, proposals, invoices, crews, scheduling, and member portal.

*Tier 3 is available on **Own it** only — not on the monthly subscription plan.*

---

## Own it — One-time purchase

| | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|--------|--------|--------|
| **Setup** | $987 | $1,777 | $4,798 |
| **Monthly hosting & support** | $50/mo | $125/mo | $250/mo |

First month of hosting & support is **free after launch**.

Includes hosting, SSL, platform updates, security fixes, and **unlimited content updates** while your monthly plan is active.

Redesigns and new features are quoted separately.

---

## Rent it — Monthly subscription

| | Tier 1 | Tier 2 |
|---|--------|--------|
| **Setup** | $200 | $200 |
| **Monthly** | $117/mo | $227/mo |

Setup fee is bank transfer only, due before build begins. Monthly fee is billed in advance (card or bank). No trial, no free month.

Site stays on our hosting. Includes **unlimited content updates** while subscribed.

You may **buy out** to Own it anytime by paying the full Tier 1 ($987) or Tier 2 ($1,777) setup fee.
